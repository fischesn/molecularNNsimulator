# DNA NN Simulator v2.1

This camera-ready artifact accompanies the GLOBECOM 2026 paper *6G Network
Empowering AI Agents*.

## Highlights

- Corrects the EIR gate/hysteresis state transition and the diffusive
  first-passage channel implementation.
- Adds 27 regression and invariant tests.
- Archives high-confidence Phase-4 sweeps with trial-level evaluation and
  calibration data.
- Adds a 54-configuration EIR robustness grid, three physical/operating
  sensitivity studies, and two scaling studies.
- Provides paired-strategy scenario seeds, disjoint calibration/evaluation
  streams, validation reports, and SHA-256 checksums.
